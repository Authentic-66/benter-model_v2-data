# Phase 3C — Feature Engineering (v1 Baseline)

_Generated by `scripts/generate_phase3c_report.py`. Feature config version: `1.0.0` (2026-07-01)._

## Headline Findings

**Pipeline runs in ~25 seconds** on the full 116,311-entry GP corpus, producing a 78-column wide table `entry_features_v1` in `gp_full.db`.

**105 features cataloged in `feature_config.json`; 73 are active in v1** (target was 55-70; we ran a little rich to give the model more to work with).

**Bucket 5 (Pedigree) is intentionally all-off.** Those features are the natural home for Doug's v10 workbook signals, which arrive in Phase 3F. Leaving them off in v1 means the baseline is market-free of pedigree — we can quantify the marginal value of v10 signals cleanly when they turn on.

**No leakage: every rolling stat is prior-only.** For an entry on 2024-06-01, features never see races on or after 2024-06-01 by the same horse/trainer/jockey/etc. Same-day races on the same card are also excluded from any entity's own history.

**Bayesian shrinkage is applied everywhere it belongs.** A rookie trainer with 1 win in 3 starts is not a 33% trainer — the shrinkage utility (`bayesian_shrinkage.py`) pulls sparse-sample rates back toward the population prior (~12% win rate) using per-cell k values.

**Computed speed figures cover 84.8% of entries.** The other 15.2% are tail-end finishers whose beaten-lengths margin the chart caller omitted — we leave the figure NULL rather than impute a nonsense number.

**Single-track redundancies to expect.** In this v1 baseline the corpus is Gulfstream Park only. Feature pairs `career_starts` / `starts_at_track` and `career_wins` / `wins_at_track` are 100% correlated by construction — every prior start IS an at-track start. These features will diverge once Phase 3H adds other tracks; we keep both in the schema now to avoid re-plumbing later.

## Bayesian shrinkage in action

Illustrating how (`wins`, `starts`) maps to a shrunk rate under `k=20`, `prior=0.12` (the trainer/jockey `overall` config):

| Wins | Starts | Raw rate | Shrunk rate |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0 | n/a | 0.120 |
| 0 | 5 | 0.000 | 0.096 |
| 1 | 5 | 0.200 | 0.136 |
| 3 | 10 | 0.300 | 0.180 |
| 5 | 20 | 0.250 | 0.185 |
| 10 | 50 | 0.200 | 0.177 |
| 30 | 200 | 0.150 | 0.147 |

A trainer with 1 win in 5 starts (20% raw) is shrunk to ~14% — close to the population prior. By 200 starts, the raw and shrunk rates converge (10% raw → 10.2% shrunk).
